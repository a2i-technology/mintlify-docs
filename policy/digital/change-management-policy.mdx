## Purpose

To ensure changes to Allied digital systems are planned, reviewed, tested, and authorised before deployment, so that security and reliability are maintained.

## Scope

Applies to changes to production digital systems, including software releases, infrastructure, and configuration. Engineering change to physical product is governed separately under the Engineering Policy.
