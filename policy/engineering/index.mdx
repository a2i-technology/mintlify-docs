## Long-form meaning and scope

**Engineering** policies cover physical engineering design authority. This domain governs how we establish and maintain technical correctness, configuration baselines, and verification logic for physical systems.

## Scope boundaries

- **Configuration management** — Design baselines, change control, versioning of engineering artefacts
- **Design assurance** — Design review, approval authorities, design freeze
- **Verification and validation (V&V)** — Test strategy, acceptance criteria, evidence of compliance
- **Design authority** — Who can approve design changes; handover to production

## What belongs here

- POL-ENG-xxx policies
- Configuration management policy
- Design assurance policy
- V&V policy
- Engineering change control (distinct from production change)

## What does not belong here

- Manufacturing execution (→ production/)
- Software change control (→ digital/)
- Enterprise document control (→ enterprise/)

## Domain distinction: Engineering vs Production

- **Engineering** = design authority, technical correctness, configuration baselines, verification logic
- **Production** = repeatable realisation of approved designs; manufacturing execution, supplier control, inspection, traceability

The tension between "change" (engineering) and "stability" (production) is productive and necessary.
