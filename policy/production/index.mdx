## Long-form meaning and scope

**Production** policies cover manufacturing and supply chain controls. This domain governs how we achieve repeatable realisation of approved designs — manufacturing execution, supplier control, inspection, and traceability.

## Scope boundaries

- **Supplier quality** — Supplier qualification, incoming inspection, supplier performance
- **Inspection** — In-process and final inspection, acceptance criteria
- **Traceability** — Lot/batch traceability, serialisation, pedigree
- **Nonconformance** — Handling of nonconforming product, disposition, corrective action
- **Manufacturing change** — Production process changes (distinct from design change)

## What belongs here

- POL-PRD-xxx policies
- Supplier quality policy
- Inspection and test policy
- Traceability policy
- Nonconformance policy

## What does not belong here

- Design authority / configuration management (→ engineering/)
- Software deployment (→ digital/)
- Enterprise quality baselines (→ enterprise/)

## Domain distinction: Engineering vs Production

- **Engineering** = design authority, technical correctness, configuration baselines, verification logic
- **Production** = repeatable realisation of approved designs; manufacturing execution, supplier control, inspection, traceability
