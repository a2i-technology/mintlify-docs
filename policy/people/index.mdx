## Long-form meaning and scope

**People** policies cover conduct, ethics, and training expectations. This domain governs how we expect people to behave and what we require them to know.

## Scope boundaries

- **Conduct** — Code of conduct, behavioural expectations
- **Ethics** — Ethical standards, conflicts of interest
- **Training** — Mandatory training, competency expectations, attestation
- **Expectations** — What we require of all staff/contractors

## What belongs here

- POL-PPL-xxx policies
- Code of conduct
- Ethics policy
- Training expectations policy

## What does not belong here

- Hiring process (→ Drive, People)
- Performance management (→ Drive, People)
- Enterprise risk (→ enterprise/)
- Information security (→ digital/ or enterprise/)
