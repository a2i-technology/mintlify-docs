Allied operates an Apple-only fleet. If you need a device for work, request one through the order form — the COS office will handle the purchase and make sure the device auto-enrols on **Apple Business Manager** and our MDM, **Iru**.

## Entitlement

Use this as a guide for what to request on the form.

| Role | Device family |
| ---- | ------------- |
| Engineers, Developers, Data Scientists, Designers — anyone who needs meaningful local compute for running models, containers, or design tools | **MacBook Pro** family |
| Non-technical generalists | **MacBook Air** or **Neo** family |
| Staff who want to delineate work and life | Allied-issued phone and SIM card — typically the entry-level iPhone (currently **iPhone 17e**) |

Right-size the spec on order — discuss with your manager or the COS office if you are unsure.

## For authorised purchasers

This section is for the COS office and anyone else authorised to buy devices on Allied's behalf. Use one of the two routes below so devices auto-enrol on Apple Business Manager and Iru. Do **not** buy from consumer channels (apple.com retail, third-party resellers) — those devices cannot be auto-enrolled and will need manual provisioning.
