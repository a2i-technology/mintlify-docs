All Allied-managed devices are protected by **Iru EDR**, deployed and monitored alongside the Iru MDM. Iru continuously monitors for known vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, and threats; keeps devices compliant with Allied security policy; and remediates issues automatically where it can.

No manual set-up is required — protection is active from the moment a device is enrolled.

## What you should do

- Accept and act on prompts from Iru (updates, configuration nudges) within 14 days — see [POL-DIG-013 System and Information Integrity Policy](../../policy/digital/system-and-information-integrity-policy).
- Report anything that looks like a real or suspected incident to the Allied security officer — see [POL-DIG-011 Incident Response Plan](../../policy/digital/incident-response-plan).
