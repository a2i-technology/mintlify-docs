How to enrol a personal device for Allied work.

> **What enrolment means.** By installing Allied's MDM software you consent to Allied being able to **see the installation status of apps on the device** and **manage certain security-related configurations**. Allied does **not** have access to internal application data (emails, documents, messages).
>
> If you would prefer a clean separation between personal and work environments, ask for an Allied-issued device instead — see [Managed device](./managed-device).

## Enrolment

> **TBD — reviewer to confirm:** Iru enrolment portal URL, the current enrolment code(s), and the up-to-date list of mandatory managed apps.

1. Open the Iru enrolment portal in Safari.
2. Enter your enrolment code.
3. Download the management profile.
4. Install via Settings:
   - **iOS** — `General > VPN & Device Management`
   - **macOS** — `General > Device Management`
5. Read and accept the terms.
6. Iru will install or take management of the mandatory apps below.

## Mandatory managed apps

These are installed (or taken under management if already installed) when you enrol.

### iOS

- Iru self-service
- Google Drive, Gmail, Google Docs, Google Calendar, Google Meet, Google Sheets, Google Slides
- ChatGPT
- Cloudflare WARP
- Notion
- Slack
- Authenticator (Authy or equivalent — see [Authenticator](./authenticator))
- Xero
- Zelt

### macOS

- Iru self-service
- Google Drive
- Cloudflare WARP
- Slack
