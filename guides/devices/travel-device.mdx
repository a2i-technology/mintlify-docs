Allied maintains a fleet of dedicated travel devices for use in higher-risk environments. The intent is a digital airgap from primary systems and activities. Before you travel, you will be issued a clean device — either new or fully wiped and freshly re-imaged.

Allow **1–3 hours** for set-up. You will need public Wi-Fi away from Allied locations (a coffee shop or library is usually fine).

## Android burner phones

- **Not** managed by Allied's MDM.
- Used primarily for tactical comms and co-ordination.
- Do not sign in to Allied services or messaging groups.
- Enable disappearing messages in Signal, Telegram, and WhatsApp.
- Keep faraday-bagged while in the UK.

## iOS and macOS travel devices

### Set up

> **Heads up:** Allied uses Google Sign-In across the productivity stack. Two-factor authentication sometimes requires confirming sign-in via another Google app (e.g. YouTube), which can require a UK-based device. Sign in to all the services and browsers you will need **while still in the UK** to avoid problems abroad.

1. On public Wi-Fi, follow the standard [Managed device](./managed-device) set-up.
2. After set-up, **forget every Wi-Fi network** stored on the device.
   - **iOS** — `Settings > Wi-Fi > ⓘ > Forget This Network`
   - **macOS** — `System Settings > Wi-Fi > … > Forget This Network`

### Deployment

> **Keep faraday-bagged** until you are well clear of the port of entry.

- **iOS** — for initial in-country travel an eSIM with a data package may be necessary. [Saily](https://saily.com/) has been tested by the team and is available in Iru's self-service catalogue.
- **iOS** — once established in country, a local SIM card is fine.
- No restrictions on usage, but exercise judgement when using in public or unsecured physical locations.
- **Always secure internet connections with the VPN** — see [VPN and zero-trust access](./vpn).
- **macOS** — prefer a full shutdown over log-out or sleep when not in use.
- Never leave the device unsecured.

### Return

> Perform these steps **before** arrival at the port of departure, then return the device to its faraday bag.

1. Request remote device erasure from the Allied IT team.
2. **iOS** — remove the SIM card.
3. Shut down.
