This guide explains how to generate a branded email signature and install it in Gmail.

## Generate your signature

Go to the [Allied email signature generator](https://app.mirosign.com/t/m2doz_zv), fill in your details, and download the HTML file.

## Install in Gmail

1. Open the downloaded HTML file in your browser.
2. Press **Cmd + A** to select all, then **Cmd + C** to copy.
3. In Gmail, click the **cog icon** and select **See all settings**.
4. Scroll to the **Signature** section and click **Create new**.
5. Click into the signature editor and press **Cmd + V** to paste.
6. Save your settings.
