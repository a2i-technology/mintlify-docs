Use this when a partner needs **isolated** Google Workspace collaboration (e.g. air-gapped or heightened security), keyed to their **partner codename** from [Codenaming](/overview/operations/portfolio/codenaming).

## Subdomain and accounts

- Create a partner sub-domain using the codename: `<code_name>.a2i.network`
- Provision Workspace users as needed, e.g. `team@<code_name>.a2i.network`

## Shared Drive

Partner-facing drives follow org naming and theming in [Set-up: Shared drive](./shared-drives#external-partner-shared-drives): **`Allied <> {partner_codename}`** (not the partner’s legal name).

For internal structure, access via Google Groups, and PARA folder layout, use the same guide.
