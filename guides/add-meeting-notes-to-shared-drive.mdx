This guide explains how to move meeting notes into a shared drive using the **Add to Liminal** Google Workspace add-on. Liminal is our internal research and intelligence tool.

## Install the add-on

1. Go to the [Add to Liminal listing](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/add_to_liminal/941626835442).
2. Install the add-on.
3. Grant all permissions the add-on requests.

## Add meeting notes to the shared drive

Open the meeting notes document in Google Docs, then open the add-on via **Extensions > Add to Liminal**. Google saves meeting notes to **My Drive > Meeting Recordings** by default. Meeting notes are stored in the [Liminal meeting notes folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1OtIw8T2eGF7wCagVJA9EGosU02xG3CwP?usp=drive_link).

### Temporary workaround for Gemini-generated documents

There is a known bug affecting documents generated with Gemini. Until this is resolved, you will need to **copy the document URL from your browser and paste it into the add-on** when prompted. This is a temporary workaround — once the bug is fixed, adding notes will return to a single-click action.
