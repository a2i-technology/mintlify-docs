**Functions** show **accountability**. Each function owns a defined set of outcomes and handoffs. They tell you who to engage for decisions and delivery. A function may be involved across multiple domains and programmes. This page is Allied’s definitive list; each function is defined below.

> - Who owns this outcome? → **Function**

## Executive

**Purpose:** Capital, top-level partnerships, commercial oversight, hiring.

**Owns:** Company-level direction, external credibility, top-level commitments.

---

## Commercial

**Purpose:** Build and manage relationships, pipeline, commitments.

**Owns:** NDAs/MOUs/contracts, pricing intent, partnership structures.

**Interfaces:** Handoff to Delivery at commitment.

---

## Delivery

**Purpose:** Execute commitments, deployments, partner/customer success.

**Owns:** Implementation, mission success, feedback loop into product/engineering.

---

## Hardware & Production

**Purpose:** Physical system design authority and manufacturing readiness.

**Engineering:** Design authority, change/configuration, verification logic.

**Production:** Supplier control, manufacturing quality, traceability.

---

## Research & Intelligence

**Purpose:** Intelligence, analysis, enabling function across org.

**Owns:** Research outputs, sensing, analytic support.

---

## Business Operations

**Purpose:** Internal tempo, operating cadence, structure and coordination.

**Owns:** Planning rhythms, systems, process design.

---

## Compliance

**Purpose:** Regulatory adherence, compliance controls, audit readiness, assurance.

**Owns:** Compliance workstreams, control evidence, external audit coordination, certification maintenance.

---

## Risk

**Purpose:** Risk posture, enterprise risk management.

**Owns:** Risk management model, risk register, risk appetite.

---

## Executive / Governance

**Purpose:** Corporate governance and oversight.

**Legal (GC):** Advisory, contracts, legal interpretation; independent.

**Finance:** Runway, budgeting, forecasting, board reporting.
