**Domains** represent what sort of work something is. They are the high-level areas of the business where different types of work happen. Policy, procedures, and templates use common domain names so the policy stack lines up with those work areas. **This page** is the definitive list and definition; policy documents for each domain sit under [Policy](../../policy).

> - What kind of work or control is this? → **Domain**.

## Allied’s domains

| Domain | ID prefix | Policy home | Scope |
|--------|------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Enterprise | `ENT` | [Enterprise](../../policy/enterprise) | Company-wide working |
| Engineering | `ENG` | [Engineering](../../policy/engineering) | Physical and product software engineering authority |
| Production | `PRD` | [Production](../../policy/production) | Manufacturing, assembly, testing |
| Digital | `DIG` | [Digital](../../policy/digital) | Software, platform, data governance |
| People | `PPL` | [People](../../policy/people) | Hiring, HR, training |

**Abbreviations** used in IDs: ENT, ENG, PRD, DIG, PPL. Conventions for policy front matter and IDs are on [Policy](../../policy).
