Drata (or similar tooling) is a compliance control and evidence management layer for tracking controls, mapping frameworks, and collecting evidence.

## Relationship to this architecture

- Git holds definitive policies and governance artefacts
- Drata references those artefacts and collects evidence from systems (Drive, SSO logs, etc.)
