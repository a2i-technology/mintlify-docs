Google Drive (Shared Drives) is the **collaborative workspace** for working documents, drafts, slides, spreadsheets, PDFs, and partner-shared materials.

## What lives in Drive

- Working documents and drafts
- Slides, spreadsheets, PDFs, diagrams
- Partner-shared materials
- Sensitive files (protected via Shared Drive access boundaries)

## What does not live in Drive (definitive)

- Policy (final → Git)
- Governance decisions (→ Git)
- Organisational structure (→ Git)

Drive may contain working drafts; final definitive versions belong in Git.

## Shared Drives (access boundaries)

- Allied – Core (broad access)
- Allied – Commercial (restricted)
- Allied – Projects (access per project)
- Allied – Finance (restricted)
- Allied – People (restricted)
- Allied - Executive (restricted)
