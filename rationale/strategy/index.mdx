## Design notes

The **Strategy** section is organised by **reading order and narrative themes** (north star → annual direction → operating concepts → production → technology → market → roadmaps), not by mirroring the **policy domain** folders (Enterprise, Engineering, Production, Digital, People). Policy domains classify **controls and templates**; strategy answers **where we are going and why**. Forcing the same folder tree for both tends to create empty buckets and blurs **intent** vs **mandatory constraint** — see working note `org-reset/org-repo-strategy-section-design-logic.md` for the full argument and external parallels (TOGAF-style separation of landscape vs standards base, COBIT governance framing, ISO documentation hierarchy — **interpretive**, not a claim of certification).

**Alignment without isomorphism:** when a strategy theme touches a domain (e.g. technology direction and digital governance), **link** to the relevant `policy/*` page in the body; do not duplicate policy requirements in strategy prose. The definitive [Strategy](/overview/strategy) index encodes this explicitly.

`org-reset/allied_operational_reset_skeleton.md` is a **feeds-into-strategy** working outline when slices are promoted; it is not itself definitive structure.
