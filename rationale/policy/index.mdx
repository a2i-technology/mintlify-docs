## Design notes

The **Policy** section is the **mandatory control stack**: `POL-*` IDs, domain codes, review metadata. It is intentionally separate from [Strategy](/strategy) (intent) and [Rationale](/rationale) (handbook design commentary). Domain folders mirror the same five domains as [Domains](/systems/org/domains) so readers can move from “kind of work” to “written obligations” predictably.

Planned validation rules for policy YAML are sketched in `org-reset/org-repo-validation-process.md`.
