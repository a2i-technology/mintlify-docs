## Design notes

**Functions** answer *who owns the outcome*; they can span domains and programmes. The definitive page groups **Hardware & Production** with nested **Engineering** and **Production** capacities to keep physical design authority and manufacturing execution in one accountable umbrella while still naming the two concerns separately — a compromise between org-chart trees and readable handbook sections.

Older architecture drafts sometimes show **Engineering** and **Production** as separate top-level tree nodes; the handbook list is the **published** shape. When reconciling legacy diagrams, update diagrams toward this page rather than silently diverging prose.

`org-reset/organisational-design.md` traces function labels across sources for consistency work.
