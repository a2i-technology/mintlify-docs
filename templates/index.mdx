Templates support procedures. They are checklists, forms, and reusable artefacts.

## Template domains

Templates mirror the policy domain structure:

| Domain | Path | Scope |
|--------|------|-------|
| Enterprise | [enterprise](./enterprise) | Company-wide baselines |
| Engineering | [engineering](./engineering) | Physical engineering |
| Production | [production](./production) | Manufacturing and supply chain |
| Digital | [digital](./digital) | Software/platform/data |
| People | [people](./people) | Conduct, ethics, training |

See [policy-index](../policy-index) for cross-domain navigation (policy · procedures · templates).

## Relationship to policy and procedures

- **Templates** are not policy
- **Templates** are not authoritative unless referenced by a procedure
- Templates support procedures; procedures may reference templates
