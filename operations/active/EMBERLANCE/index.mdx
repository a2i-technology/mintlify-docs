## Intent

UK government R&D contract supporting our Ukrainian partner Airlogix to develop a new capability (Dark Horizon, DH). If EMBERLANCE results in a successful operational capability, UK MOD may place an order for DH to gift to Ukraine. Allied would produce units in the UK through the Joint Venture with Airlogix.

## Scope

**In scope:**
- R&D work associated with EMBERLANCE contract
- Support for Airlogix DH development and testing
- JV establishment for scale-up
- Export licence preparation (Ukrainian government)

**Out of scope:**
- Production of other platforms (e.g. Anubis) unless EMBERLANCE path fails

## Success Criteria

- Airlogix completes DH development and testing
- JV established
- Ukrainian export licence obtained
- UK MOD order for DH (or alternative route to production)
